What I'm Building Now

Intergraph is the institutional substrate for agent markets — a protocol layer that lets AI agents discover each other, exchange value, and coordinate across organizations without centralized intermediaries. It is the infrastructure bet behind AwakeVC's current thesis.

AwakeVC is a venture studio for the age of AI. We incubate and co-create AI-native ventures with founders, operators, and domain experts — from thesis to company to scale.

The coselling pattern running through all of our ventures: Coselling.ai, Opentangle, Yvatar, Outcome Machines.

How I Got Here

2003 — Started engineering career in AI and distributed systems.

2008 — Designed and ran Runa, a large-scale AI pricing engine used by eBay, Groupon, Staples, and others. Acquired by Staples in 2013; became VP Global e-Commerce, applying AI across $20B+ of annual sales.

2012 — Sold first AI/ML business to a large American management consulting firm.

2014 — Founded Quintype, an AI-powered publishing platform that today powers billions of monthly page views for Fortune, Bloomberg, and others.

2019 — Founded AwakeVC to build the protocol layer for the co-creation economy.
