Decision Intelligence

Decision intelligence is the discipline of turning data, context, and goals into reliably good decisions — at the speed and scale that AI-native organizations require.

Outcome Machines

Outcome Machines applies decision intelligence to enterprise data. It takes the sprawl of metrics, models, and dashboards that most enterprises have accumulated, and connects them to measurable business outcomes — so that every decision is evaluated not by its inputs but by its results.

Outcomeering

Outcomeering is the methodology: a systematic way to define, track, and engineer outcomes rather than just activities. Where traditional project management asks "are we on schedule?", Outcomeering asks "are we producing the outcome we committed to?"

Decision Intelligence in the Agentic Economy

As AI agents take over execution, the human role in organizations shifts toward decision-making at a higher level of abstraction. Decision intelligence is the capability that lets humans set the right goals, evaluate agent performance against outcomes, and course-correct before small deviations become large failures. It is the governance layer for the agentic economy.
