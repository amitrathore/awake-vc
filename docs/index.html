How it works

You bring the domain.

Deep knowledge of a market, its customers and its pain.

I bring the system.

Agents, distribution, formation and a cofounder who has done this many times.

We build it together.

I take a minority stake and work alongside you from thesis to launch.

The system

Every venture in the studio runs on four shared layers.

Agents

A shared stack of AI agents, memory and rules. Your company starts with a working team on day one.

Distribution

One coseller network across every venture. People who help a sale happen get paid, whatever the brand.

Formation

A standard cofounder agreement and launch playbook. No reinventing the legal and operational basics.

Capital

A path from first revenue to outside investment once the company has traction.

Why it compounds

Most companies get harder to run as they grow. This system gets easier. The tenth venture inherits everything the first nine built: agents, cosellers, contracts and lessons.

That's the bet behind AwakeVC. In the age of agents, one person's reach is no longer fixed by headcount. It compounds.
